EverGreen started with a simple idea: blending the purity of nature with the precision of AI to redefine the grocery experience.

AI-Optimized Inventory

Beyond standard stock management, we are developing predictive algorithms to handle real-time stock reconciliation. By analyzing purchase patterns, EverGreen reduces food waste by ensuring we only stock what our community truly needs.

Dynamic Weight Logic

Nature doesn't come in standard sizes. Our system utilizes custom logic to handle weight-based pricing for organic produce, ensuring you pay exactly for what you receive—powered by backend precision in Java and MySQL.
